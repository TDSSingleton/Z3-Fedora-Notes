stage14 -- DisplayPort over USB-C working, 1080p, automatic on attach
=====================================================================
Taken 2026-11-20, after the first session in which the TV showed a correct
picture. This is the restore point for "external display works".

WHAT WORKS HERE
  * DP alt mode over USB-C on a powered Nintendo Switch dock (VID 0x057e,
    PID 0x2003): full VDM handshake, pin assignment D, 2 lanes.
  * Link trains at HBR2 -- rate = 540000, bw_code = 20, and v_level = 0 with
    p_level = 0, i.e. at the LOWEST drive strength. The link is not straining.
  * 1920x1080 (at 50 Hz; the TV's EDID offers no 1080p60) with KWin driving
    the TV as the primary output and the inner panel extended beside it.
    Both DSI panels stay live.
  * Automatic: plug the dock in and the picture appears with no commands.
    Measured 2 s from CC attach to "DP IS UP". Works at boot too (coldplug).
  * Charging from the dock continues while DP is up, at 500 mA.

THE ONE THING THAT MADE IT WORK
  Waiting for the SINK's own HPD (HPD_State, bit 7 of a DP Status Update VDM)
  instead of forcing our software HPD. Forcing ours while the sink reported 0
  made msm_dp train a link into a dock whose HDMI side was not ready; the TV
  then showed its own blue no-signal screen. That blue screen was blamed on
  widebus, on DPU mixer staging and on Plasma for an entire session. No kernel
  change was needed -- only the order of operations.

TO RESTORE
  scp q2q-boot-FLASHED-20261120.img root@<ip>:/root/boot-new.img
  ssh root@<ip> 'S=$(stat -c%s /root/boot-new.img);
    dd if=/root/boot-new.img of=/dev/sda19 bs=4M conv=fsync; sync
    echo 3 > /proc/sys/vm/drop_caches
    head -c $S /dev/sda19 | sha256sum'   # must match the sha256 above
  then copy dp/* into /usr/local/bin (and the .rules/.service to their dirs)

ORDER OF OPERATIONS THAT MATTERS ON THE HARDWARE
  The dock's power brick goes in BEFORE the phone. A dock with no brick
  presents Rd and asks the PHONE to power IT (CCStat = 2 = cc_SOURCE,
  VBUSDet = 0), and then Discover Identity ACKs with an EMPTY VDM, which
  looks exactly like a broken adapter. The dock latches this at attach and
  does not re-evaluate when the brick arrives later -- replug the phone.
